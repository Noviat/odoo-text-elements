========================
Text Elements - Purchase
========================

Text elements on requests for quotation and purchase orders.

Print the texts of the catalogue on requests for quotation and purchase orders:
delivery instructions for your suppliers, the reference to mention on their
delivery note, your purchasing conditions.

This module adds the **Text Elements** tab to the form and prints the texts at the
position chosen in the catalogue: top of document, before lines, after totals or
bottom of document, in the customer's language.

.. image:: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Noviat/odoo-text-elements/18.0/text_element_purchase/static/description/purchase_order_report.png
   :alt: A request for quotation with delivery instructions before the lines

Requirements
============

**Text Elements** (``text_element_base``), which holds the catalogue, the
conditions, the dynamic placeholders and the preview. See its README for the
configuration.

Usage
=====

Open the **Text Elements** tab of a document to see the texts that will be
printed, add texts from the catalogue, personalise one, or write a free text.
