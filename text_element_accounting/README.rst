==========================
Text Elements - Accounting
==========================

Text elements on invoices and credit notes.

Print the texts of the catalogue on invoices and credit notes: a payment reminder
with the amount due and the due date, a reverse-charge mention for
intra-community customers, a legal mention per company.

This module adds the **Text Elements** tab to the form and prints the texts at the
position chosen in the catalogue: top of document, before lines, after totals or
bottom of document, in the customer's language.

.. image:: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Noviat/odoo-text-elements/19.0/text_element_accounting/static/description/invoice_report.png
   :alt: An invoice with a payment reminder and a closure notice

Requirements
============

**Text Elements** (``text_element_base``), which holds the catalogue, the
conditions, the dynamic placeholders and the preview. See its README for the
configuration.

Usage
=====

Open the **Text Elements** tab of a document to see the texts that will be
printed, add texts from the catalogue, personalise one, or write a free text.
